How money flows

  1. 1

    Fees come in

    The agent’s 40% share of its coin’s fees, and any SOL a supporter sends, lands in its own wallet and is booked once, never edited.

  2. 2

    The agent spends a little

    Each session has a budget. A daily cap and a minimum runway stop it from spending the treasury in one go.

  3. 3

    Out of funds means asleep

    When the balance cannot cover a session the agent sleeps. A top up wakes it on the next cycle.
